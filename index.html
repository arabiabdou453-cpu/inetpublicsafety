Next Generation CAD/Mobile Solutions

The iNet Public Safety solution truly is the next step in technology. Our Enterprise Class, Map-Centric CAD and Mobile are powerful solutions developed with ease of use in mind. Seamless integration between CAD, Mobile and other interfaced 3rd party systems provides for optimal workflow and maximum situational awareness for dispatchers and in-field personnel. CAD & Mobile have been designed from the gound up to support remote access over the internet natively or by using VPN thereby providing agencies with the maximum operational flexibility and convenience. Any authorized computer whether it be at your PSAP, EOC or in the field, can quickly access the system via LAN, WAN, Cell, WiFi. Automated application and map updates help reduce the load on your IT personnel. With existing sites of from 1 to 210+ active CAD stations, the iNet solution has proven scalability, redundancy and performance. The power, flexibility and ability to scale to very large sites makes the iNet solution a perfect choice for country, state/province wide deployments to small single position sites.

Next Generation 911 (NG911) Call Handling Solution

The iNet911 CHS provides 911 primary and secondary PSAPs with a redundant NG911 call handling solution that can also operate under E911. The CHS includes a software phone console (iNetPhone softphone) which is powerful, intuitive and simple to operate. Integration with other (non-iNet) CAD systems is accomplished via the standard NENA interface specification for CAD interfaces or via the NENA I3 specifications for NG911 Call Handling Systems. We have many installations in operation with 3rd Party CHS products integrated to our CAD. For sites using iNetCAD in addition to iNet911 CHS, integration comes out of the box and provides for many additional integration features such as for example a single shared map with CAD, initiating calls from iNetCAD and more.
